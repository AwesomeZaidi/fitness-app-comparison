# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, argue about evidence rather than people, and assume good faith — including from people who work on one of the apps being compared. Harassment, personal attacks and publishing others' private information are not tolerated.

Disagreeing with a value is welcome; that is what this repository is for. Do it with a source.

**Reporting.** Email splyt.fit@gmail.com with "Code of conduct" in the subject. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential. If the report concerns the maintainer, say so and it will be handled by someone else at SPLYT.
